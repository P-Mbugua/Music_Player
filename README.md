# Music Player Web Application

Welcome to the Music Player web application! This project allows you to discover, play, and enjoy your favorite songs directly from your device. You can choose songs from your files, create playlists, and easily navigate through your music collection. The player has a modern and responsive design, optimized for all devices.

## Features

- **Play Music:** Choose songs from your device and play them.
- **Responsive UI:** Designed to work on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Song List:** Displays a list of songs from your device, and allows you to choose and play any song.
- **Controls:** Play, pause, next, and previous song controls.
- **Sticky Header:** A header that remains fixed at the top of the page for easy access to controls.
- **Footer:** Includes a footer with a link to [Mbugua Peter's website](https://mbuguapeter.netlify.app).

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: The basic structure of the webpage.
- **CSS3**: Styling the webpage with a responsive design and official fonts.
- **JavaScript**: Handling the music player functionality and interactions.
